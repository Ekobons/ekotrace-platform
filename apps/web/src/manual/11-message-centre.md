# Message centre

Questions to the Ekotrace team, and the team's announcements. Menu → Capture → Inbox & messages → **Message centre**. The count on the menu item is your unread replies and announcements.

## Conversations

**+ New message:** pick a **topic** (question, help entering data, something not working, idea or feature request, emission factors & methods, subscription & billing), the **part of Ekotrace** it is about, a subject and the message. Attach a screenshot or a file if it helps (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, PDF, XLSX, CSV, DOCX, TXT; up to 10 MB). Never send passwords or access codes.

```flow
> Waiting for the team | sent; the team sees it in its inbox
> Answered | the team replied — the menu shows a count until you open it
> Closed | you marked it resolved, or the team closed it; writing again reopens it
```

You see your own conversations. The **super admin** sees every conversation of the company (the unread count is only for their own).

## Announcements

News from the team: new emission factors, new features, planned maintenance, regulation. Each person marks them read (one at a time or all at once). An announcement may be for every company or only for some.

## For the Ekotrace team (platform admin)

**Messages** (under Platform console) is the team inbox: every company's conversations, **Needs reply** first, with the company, the person, their role and email, and the page it is about. **Send reply** (marks it answered), **Send & close**, **Close without reply** or **Reopen**.

The **Announcements** tab publishes to every company or to the companies ticked, with an optional end date, and shows how many people have read each one; **Withdraw** takes one down.
