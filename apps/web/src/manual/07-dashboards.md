# Dashboards

Dashboards (top of the menu) shows the year's inventory for the whole group, an entity or sub-group, or one facility.

## Filters

Along the top: **year**; **months** (the year so far, a quarter, or one month); **compare with** the previous year, the base year, or nothing; **Scope 2** location- or market-based; and **which part of the group**. The comparison always uses the same months, so January–September 2026 is compared with January–September 2025. **Export** downloads the figures behind the view (CSV); **Share view** copies a link that opens the same view.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | the total (dark box) with the change against the comparison year and the base year, the split by scope; per employee, data confidence (actual vs estimated) and approvals; monthly columns by scope with the comparison year as a dashed line (monthly or cumulative, all scopes or one); **Where** — the parts of what you view, click one to go into it, ↑ to go back up; **What** — the biggest sources; tiles for Energy, Water, Waste and Mobility; **What moved** |
| Emissions | totals and each scope with their change; monthly columns by scope; by entity & facility (facilities under their entity); by GHG Protocol category with its code; category × month table; purchases by product group, top suppliers and the supplier-specific share |
| Energy | energy used (kWh, MWh, GWh), from fuels, electricity, renewable share (certificates claimed), well-to-tank and grid losses; energy-related emissions per month; fuel by form, electricity & cooling by source, well-to-tank by source, energy by facility and per m², top fuels by quantity |
| Water | not captured yet — comes with the water module |
| Waste | waste from our operations (t), share diverted from landfill, emissions from waste, waste treated at own sites, biogenic CO₂; emissions per month by route; by stream, where it goes, diversion by facility |
| Mobility & travel | fleet and business travel, distance driven, g CO₂e per km, electric & hybrid share; by vehicle type, by fuel, per km, business travel by kind, by facility |

Each tab ends with **What moved**: the three sources that changed most against the comparison year, with the facility where most of the change happened — calculated from the entries, not written by hand.

## How totals are counted

- **Consolidation:** operational or financial control (a facility counts in full or not at all) or equity share (× the ownership percentage), as set in Methodology & boundaries.
- Rejected entries are left out; entries waiting for approval count.
- People see only the facilities they have access to.
- Purchases count once published.
- Entries for a whole year (e.g. landfill methane) count in the full-year view and its totals, not in the months, quarters or single months.
- **Energy** is the quantity entered when it is energy (kWh, MWh, TRh of cooling); fuels by litre, kg or m³ are converted with the calorific value behind their factors. Vehicles entered by distance count in emissions but not in energy (the tab says how many).
- Figures are shown in t or kt (thousand tonnes), whichever fits the tab.
