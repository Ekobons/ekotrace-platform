# Targets & actions

**Targets & actions** (menu → Net zero) is the net-zero plan in one place: the targets, how they line up with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), scenarios, the reduction actions with their costs, and progress against the path. It uses the **VIEWING** and **PERIOD** buttons at the top like the dashboards: the numbers are for the company, sub-group, facility or group viewed.

Everyone can look. **Managers and above** add and change actions and scenarios (for the whole company, or a facility they can see). The **super admin** changes the targets, the SBTi structure and the Net Zero year.

## How the numbers are worked out

- **Base year** — the full base year (Methodology & boundaries), approved data.
- **Run-rate** — this year's approved emissions for the months so far, annualised (× 12 ÷ months). An entry covering several months (a landfill model, the commuting year) is annualised by the months it covers. A yearly source not entered yet this year carries the base-year figure until it is.
- **Target path** — a straight line from the base year to the target year, separately for Scope 1+2 and Scope 3.
- **Projection for this year** — the months so far scaled by the base year's monthly pattern.
- **Business as usual** — the run-rate growing by the business growth % a year (SBTi setup).

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Target register | The target, the reduction so far (Scope 1+2, same months as the base year), committed abatement and approved investment; the SBTi-compatible target structure; the scenarios compared; objectives (the two main SBTi targets plus any of your own); the largest actions; then the targets themselves with the Scope 1+2 and Scope 3 paths and each facility's Scope 1+2 against its path |
| SBTi setup | Target type, scopes, coverage, ambition, target year, business growth and validation status; a **readiness check** against the SBTi near-term criteria (4.2% a year for Scope 1+2, Scope 3 target when Scope 3 is 40% or more, 67% coverage or supplier engagement, 5–10 years, base year from 2015, inventory at least 90% approved). Indicative — SBTi validates the submission |
| Scenario analysis | Each scenario's actions, abatement, projected emissions in the target year, gap to the target, capex and NPV; the **scenario lab**: levers (renewables, fleet electrified, efficiency, refrigerant leaks, landfill diversion, growth) phased in evenly to the target year, with the path against the target. Lever settings can be saved and loaded |
| Action portfolio | The **action library** (energy, fleet, refrigerants, waste, purchases, travel & commute, water) sized from your own data, plus your custom actions. **Select** puts an action in the scenario chosen; **Details** sets its KPI, metric value, period, owner, status, facility, planned and actual reduction, costs and savings, or excludes it |
| ROI & cost | Per action: CapEx, OpEx, saving, payback, ROI over its life, total cost of ownership, NPV at 8% and net cost per tonne (negative = it pays for itself); scenarios compared; the **marginal abatement cost curve** |
| Tracking | **Net Zero trajectory** from the base year through the near-term target to Net Zero (year and residual % set here); the **projection** from this year's run-rate, growth and each year's reductions to the target year; **delivery tracking**; **estimated vs actual** reduction for actions approved or under way |

## Reductions

An action's reduction = the run-rate of the activities it affects × its typical reduction × the metric value (how far it is adopted). Library figures are indicative starting points; replace them with your own under Details. Changing the metric value of a library action recalculates its planned reduction. An action ramps up evenly from its start year to its end year.

**Scenarios** — every company has a **Current plan**. **Create scenario** adds another, optionally starting from the current plan's actions; select actions into it in Action portfolio. The current plan cannot be removed.

**Import SBTi template** (super admin) sets the main targets from an SBTi pathway and a target year: near-term 1.5 °C (4.2% a year Scope 1+2, 2.5% Scope 3), well-below 2 °C, the Net-Zero Standard (adds −90% all scopes by 2050) or supplier engagement (adds a 67% supplier objective).

Actions **done** show as **Reductions delivered** on Home and on the Net zero dashboard (the measured reduction when entered, otherwise the planned one).
