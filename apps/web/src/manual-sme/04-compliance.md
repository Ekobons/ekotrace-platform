# Compliance

Dates, checklists and duties to employees.

## Calendar

Every compliance date for your company: PF and ESI, licence renewals, pollution board returns, POSH report, CSR, CBAM, certificate renewals, and your own dates.

- The tiles count **overdue**, **due within 45 days**, **later** and **done**.
- Filter by status; items are grouped by month.
- Tick **Done** when filed.
- Enter your **Consent to Operate**, **factory licence** and **fire NOC** expiry dates so their renewals appear. Certificate expiry dates come from **Certifications**.

**Your own dates.** Press **+ Add a date** to add anything else you must not miss: a boiler inspection, an insurance renewal, a buyer audit. Give it a name and a date, choose **Once** or **Every year**, and add a note if you like (who to call, what to bring). It appears in the list marked *Your date*. Use **Change** or **Remove** to edit it.

**Add to my calendar.** The button at the top downloads every upcoming date as one calendar file. Open it on your phone or computer to add the dates to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar; each comes with an alert 3 days before, and monthly and yearly dates repeat. The file is a copy: after you add or change dates, download it again.

**E-mail reminders.** The owner, an admin or a manager chooses:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Send reminder e-mails | switches the reminders on or off |
| Who receives them | any of the people in your company |
| Days before the date | 30, 15, 7, 3 or 1 days; 15 and 3 to start with |
| Also once when a date has passed | one more e-mail the day after a date that is not ticked done |

Press **Save**. Reminders go out each morning. Every reminder is sent once only, with all the dates due in one e-mail per person; a date ticked **Done** gets no more reminders. **Last sent** shows the most recent ones.

If the card says **E-mail not set up**, the server cannot send e-mail yet: your choices are kept and reminders start once support sets up e-mail. Until then, use **Add to my calendar**.

WhatsApp reminders are not sent automatically; ready-made messages to send yourself are on the **WhatsApp** page.

## EPR

Extended producer responsibility for plastic packaging, e-waste and batteries. Turn on **We use plastic packaging**, then enter your role, CPCB registration, tonnes by plastic category, the recycling target and certificates bought. The page shows certificates bought against those needed, what to do next and the annual return date.

## DPDP

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to every business holding personal data — employees, customers, vendors, CCTV. The main duties apply from **13 May 2027**. The page has five tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Overview | where you stand in each of seven areas, the next step, key dates, what the law asks and the penalties |
| Data register | one entry per kind of data: what you hold, why, on what basis, where it is kept, who can see it, how long you keep it and which vendors handle it |
| Checklist | 25 steps in seven areas (know your data, notice and consent, use and deletion, security, breaches, people's rights, vendors). Open a step to see why it matters and the evidence to keep; record the owner, the date and a note, and attach the evidence itself |
| Breaches & requests | the **breach log** (with a 72-hour clock to report to the Data Protection Board) and the **request log** (people asking to see, correct or erase their data, with a 90-day clock) |
| Ready texts | a privacy notice (English and Hindi), a notice to employees, a CCTV signboard, a consent wording, a vendor contract clause and a breach report to the Board — made from your register; copy or print |

Start with the **Data register**, beginning with employees. Employee data used for employment needs no separate consent (a "legitimate use" under section 7); choose the basis for each kind of data.

> The ready texts are a starting point. Review them with your lawyer or CA before you use them.

## People & safety

- **Workforce:** employees, share of women, training hours.
- **Safety log:** add each injury with its date and days lost. The page works out lost-time injuries and the LTIFR (injuries per million hours worked). If there were none, switch on **No incidents this year**.
- **POSH:** Internal Committee, external member, annual report. Attach the filed report under it.
- **Safety records:** attach your accident register, first-aid register or safety audit under the safety log.
- **Workers' grievance QR:** print the poster (English, Hindi and Marathi) and put it up on the shop floor. A worker scans it and writes about a problem without signing in and without giving a name. No phone number or IP address is kept.
- **Grievances:** the messages received. A manager, admin or the owner marks them resolved or reopens them.

## CSR

Whether the CSR rule (Companies Act section 135) applies — net worth, turnover or net profit above the thresholds — your 2% obligation, what you have spent and what is left, with notes on the committee and deadlines.
