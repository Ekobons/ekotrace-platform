# Getting started

Ekotrace SME helps a small or medium Indian company understand its carbon footprint, its ESG readiness and the rules that apply to it, find money it can save, and share its figures with buyers, banks and its CA — at a nominal fee, in plain language.

> Figures are estimates to help you plan and to answer buyers. They are not a statutory return, an audit or legal advice.

## Signing in

Open the Ekotrace SME sign-in page (the address ends in **/sme**) and sign in with your e-mail and password. The first time, you are asked to choose your own password.

New company? Use **Create your company's account** on the sign-in page (when your provider has opened self sign-up), or ask your industry association, buyer or bank for an invitation.

Forgot your password? The owner of your company account (super admin) can reset it under **People & access**.

## The screen

```flow
> Menu (left) | Home and six sections: Carbon & ESG, Save money, Compliance, Exports, Quick tools, Share
> Section bar (top) | the pages of the section you are in, as tabs; the open page is coloured
> Page | figures at the top, then the questions; results update as you type
```

On a phone, the menu opens from the **☰** button at the top left, and the page tabs scroll sideways.

Each section has its own colour: green for Carbon & ESG, amber for Save money, blue for Compliance, violet for Exports, olive for Quick tools, rose for Share.

**Pages that do not apply to you are tucked away.** For example, if your Profile says you do not export to the EU or UK, the CBAM page is hidden behind **"+1 not for you"** in the section bar. Click it to see the page anyway.

## Home

Home shows, from top to bottom:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Company band | your company, the financial year, Scope 1 + 2 emissions, emissions intensity, savings found and your ESG readiness |
| Your next steps | five steps to get going, with the next one highlighted; it disappears when all five are done |
| Sections | a tab per section; the open tab lists its pages with where each one stands (e.g. *Emissions · 540 tCO₂e*) |
| Due next | the next compliance dates |
| Money on the table | savings found by the bill analyser, solar, scrap and carbon credits |
| Where emissions come from, Readiness level | your biggest sources, your ESG level and SRMM score |
| Needs attention | the four most important rules for you, with **Everything that applies** for the full list |

## The five steps

1. **Tell us about your company** (Carbon & ESG → Profile): six answers decide which rules apply.
2. **Add your yearly bills** (Carbon & ESG → Emissions): electricity, diesel, LPG and others give your footprint.
3. **Answer the ESG questions** (Carbon & ESG → ESG): water, waste, people and policies.
4. **Check your power bill** (Save money → Bill analyser): most factories find penalties they can stop paying.
5. **Score your SRMM level** (Carbon & ESG → SRMM): the ICAI maturity level buyers and banks ask for.

Each takes a few minutes. You can stop at any time; everything is saved.

## Saving

There is no Save button: **every change saves itself** a moment after you stop typing. The heading of the page shows *Saving…*, then *Saved*. If it shows *Not saved*, check your connection and type the figure again.

If two people change the same page at once, the second person sees a message and the latest figures.

## Proof documents

Where a figure or a tick needs proof, the page has an **Attach** button: certificates (**Certifications**), signed policies (**ESG**), the POSH annual report and safety records (**People & safety**), and the evidence for each **DPDP** checklist step. Attach a PDF, a photo (JPG or PNG), or a Word or Excel file, up to 10 MB; on a computer you can also drag the file onto the line. Click a file's name to open it, and **✕** to remove it. Pages show how many items have proof, for example *2 of 7 policies have proof*.

Files are kept with the financial year shown at the top of the page. Anyone in your company can open them; data entry and above can add or remove them.

## The financial year

Everything is kept per Indian financial year, April to March (for example *FY 2025-26*). The year you are working on is shown at the top of every page. Change it, or start a new year, on **Profile**. Last year's figures stay, so you can see the trend.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner (super admin) | everything, including company details, sharing consent, the anonymous benchmark and asking a CA to validate |
| Admin | everything except sharing consent and CA requests |
| Manager | enter and change data; resolve worker grievances |
| Data entry (preparer) | enter and change data |
| Viewer (verifier) | see everything, change nothing |

The owner adds colleagues under **People & access**. Every change is recorded under **Security & audit log**.

## Plan & billing

**Plan & billing** (under Setup, for the owner and admins) shows your plan, the date your access runs until, and the plans with their yearly price including GST.

- **Paying online.** Choose a plan and press **Pay**. A secure Razorpay window opens: pay by UPI, card or net banking. We never see your card or bank details. When the payment goes through, your access is extended straight away. If you close the window too early, the payment is still recorded when the bank confirms it; do not pay twice.
- **Paying early loses nothing.** One year is added after your current end date (or from today if it has passed).
- **After the end date** nobody in the company can sign in until the plan is renewed, so pay before it.
- **If the page says online payment is not switched on**, write to the e-mail address shown on the page. We send bank details for a transfer and extend your access when the money arrives.
- **Invoices.** Every payment has a GST tax invoice: press **Invoice** in the payments list, then **Print or save as PDF**. Add your GSTIN and billing address under **Invoice details** before paying, so they appear on the invoice and you can claim the input tax credit. GST is CGST + SGST when your company is in the same state as ours, IGST otherwise. Check the GST treatment with your CA.
- Only the owner or an admin can pay or change the invoice details. Others can see the page but not change it.
