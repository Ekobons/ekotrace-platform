# Quick tools

## Bill scan

Read an electricity, diesel or LPG bill **PDF** and turn it into figures.

1. Choose the type of bill.
2. Choose or drop the PDF (up to 15 MB). Scanned PDFs are read too, a little more slowly. Photos are not supported yet.
3. Each value read is shown with the line it came from. Correct anything wrong.
4. Press **Use these figures**. Electricity goes to the Bill analyser (and, if you choose, to Emissions); diesel and LPG are added to Emissions.

## Questionnaire

Buyers often send ESG questionnaires. Paste the questions, one per line. Answers appear from your own data, marked **answered** or **needs you**. Copy one answer, or **Copy all** (questions you must answer yourself are marked *[To answer]*).

If an answer is wrong, correct the figure on its page (for example Emissions or ESG) and the answer changes.

## WhatsApp

Prepares a monthly summary or a due-date reminder, in English or Hindi, to send to your team on WhatsApp. **Copy message**, or **Open in WhatsApp** to send it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically yet.
