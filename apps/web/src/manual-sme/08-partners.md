# For buyers, clusters, banks and CA firms

Partners sign in at the normal Ekotrace address and open **SME network**. What you see depends on the kind of partner your account is set up as. You see a company's figures only while that company keeps sharing switched on.

The year picker at the top chooses the financial year.

## Buyers

**Your suppliers:** the SMEs linked to you, sorted by their share of your purchases, with their emissions, intensity and levels when they share.

- Tiles show suppliers linked, coverage (share of purchase value that has reported), reported Scope 1 + 2 and how many are not yet sharing.
- Edit the **share of purchases** in the table.
- **Remind** a supplier who has not switched sharing on (once every three days).
- **Invite a supplier** by e-mail. If the company is new to Ekotrace, an account is created and a temporary password is shown **once** — give it to them; they sign in at the SME sign-in page.

## Clusters and associations

Totals for your members, never one company on its own. Figures appear once **five or more** members share. You see the median and spread of emissions intensity, a chart of members by intensity, the biggest emission sources, how many members are at Level 2 or above, savings found, rooftop solar and power-factor problems. Invite members by e-mail.

## Banks

Your SME borrowers, with loan amount and equity plus debt (both editable), attribution, Scope 1 + 2, **financed emissions** (PCAF method) and a data quality score, with totals. Invite borrowers by e-mail.

## CA firms

The SRMM validation requests assigned to your firm. Open one to see the company's answers by section, where its data suggests a different score, the scoring rules and each section against the 30% floor. Then:

- **Mark in review** while you work,
- **Validate** with the validated score (and confirm all sections are at 30% or more) — the level is shown before you confirm,
- or **Return** it with a note saying what to fix.
