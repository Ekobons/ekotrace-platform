# Questions and words

## Common questions

**Is my data private?** Yes. Only people in your company see it. A partner sees your figures only while your owner keeps sharing switched on for that partner.

**Are the figures official?** They are careful estimates using Indian grid factors (CEA), IPCC and DEFRA fuel factors and industry averages. They are good for planning, answering buyers and bank discussions. Your SRMM level becomes official after a CA validates it.

**I made a mistake. Can I undo it?** Type the right figure; it saves itself. Earlier values are kept in the audit log.

**Some pages are missing.** Pages that do not apply to you are tucked behind *"+n not for you"* in the section bar. Check your answers on Profile.

**How do I add a colleague?** The owner or an admin opens **People & access**.

## Words used

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scope 1 | emissions from fuel you burn and refrigerant leaks |
| Scope 2 | emissions from the electricity you buy |
| Scope 3 | emissions in your value chain: materials bought, freight, travel |
| tCO₂e | tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent |
| Intensity | tonnes of CO₂e per ₹ crore of turnover |
| ESG | environment, social and governance |
| SRMM | ICAI's Sustainability Reporting Maturity Model (levels 1–4) |
| BRSR | SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, filed by listed companies, who ask suppliers for data |
| CBAM | the EU's (and from 2027 the UK's) carbon charge on imports |
| EUDR | the EU Deforestation Regulation |
| EPR | extended producer responsibility for plastic, e-waste and batteries |
| DPDP | India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act |
| POSH | the Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the workplace law |
| LTIFR | lost-time injuries per million hours worked |
| Contract demand | the electricity load (kVA) agreed with your DISCOM |
| Power factor | how much of the power drawn does useful work; below the DISCOM's limit a penalty applies |
| Udyam | the government's MSME registration |
