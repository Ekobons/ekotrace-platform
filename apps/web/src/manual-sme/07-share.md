# Share

Your data stays yours. Nothing is shared with anyone until the owner switches sharing on.

## Share & validate

**Partners** are the buyers, industry clusters, banks and CA firms linked to your company — by your provider, or because they invited you. For each partner the page shows what they see now and what they would see with sharing on.

| Partner | Sees when sharing is on |
|---|---|
| Buyer | your summary figures (emissions, intensity, ESG and SRMM levels) for its value-chain reporting |
| Bank | your figures for green loans and its financed-emissions reporting |
| Cluster | only totals of five or more members — never your company on its own |
| CA firm | your SRMM answers, and only for a validation it has been assigned |

Only the **owner** can switch sharing on or off for each partner. You can switch it off at any time.

**Anonymous benchmark:** with the owner's consent, your figures join the Indian SME emissions database, shown only as medians of five or more companies in the same industry. If you share with a cluster, stay in the benchmark too, or the cluster leaves you out.

## CA validation of your SRMM level

When every SRMM section has at least 30% of its points, the owner can **Ask a CA to validate**. Choose whether your validated score may be shown on ICAI's public list. The Ekotrace team assigns a CA firm; you see the status (requested, assigned, in review, validated or returned with a note). If you are not yet eligible, the page shows which sections are short and by how many points.

## Green loan

A bank-ready pack for a green loan: choose what the loan is for (rooftop solar, power-factor correction, all bill fixes, or another project), the amount, the repayment period and the bank. The page shows what a bank will look at — whether the yearly saving covers the repayment, your ESG and SRMM levels, Udyam registration — and the pack itself: company, emissions, savings, levels, eligible schemes and signature lines.

**Print or save as PDF** to take it to your bank.
