# Carbon & ESG

This section holds the core of your data: who you are, which rules apply, your carbon footprint, your ESG readiness and your ICAI SRMM level.

## Profile

Your company details (name, manufacturing or services, industry, state, Udyam number) and a few questions about your business:

- **Size:** employees, turnover (₹ crore) and, for manufacturers, yearly production. These give your MSME class (micro, small or medium).
- **Customers and exports:** whether you sell to listed companies, export to the EU or UK, and your main export product.
- **Products:** whether you use plastic packaging, make electrical or electronic goods or batteries.

**Plants and sites:** most companies have one site. If you have more than one plant, add each with its city and state; Emissions then lets you enter figures per plant and shows the split. Plants are kept per financial year.

These answers decide what you see under **What applies** and which pages are tucked away. Only the owner or an admin can change the company details.

The financial year switcher is at the top of this page.

## What applies

The rules that matter for your company, grouped as:

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Act now | applies to you today |
| Check soon | likely to apply, or worth preparing for |
| Keep an eye on | for information; may apply later |
| In order | nothing to do |

Each rule gives the rule in plain words, **Why it shows** (the answer that brought it in, for example "You have 85 workers") and **Next** (the next step, with a link to the page that helps). The page has three tabs.

**What applies** checks, among others:

| Rule | Shows when |
|---|---|
| Labour Codes (in force from 21 Nov 2025): appointment letters, wage slips, registers | you have employees; registering the establishment from 10 workers |
| PF, ESI and gratuity | ESI from 10 employees, EPF from 20 |
| Factory licence (OSH Code) | manufacturing with 20 or more workers using power, or 40 without |
| Consent to Establish and Operate | manufacturing; depends on your red, orange, green or white category and your Consent to Operate expiry date |
| Hazardous waste authorisation and Form 4 return | you generate hazardous waste |
| BIS Quality Control Order (ISI mark) | your product is covered, or you are not sure |
| Udyam registration | always; asks you to register if not marked |
| Plastic, e-waste and battery EPR | you marked those products; the status follows your EPR page |
| EU and UK CBAM, EUDR, BRSR value-chain | from your export and customer answers |
| CSR, DPDP, POSH, SRMM, CCTS, bank ESG checks | from your figures and answers |

**What changed** lists recent rule changes (with the date and a link to the source) that concern a business like yours, based on your sector, state and answers. **Show all updates** shows the others too, greyed.

**Your answers** asks the few questions the rules need that Profile does not: your pollution control category, your Consent to Operate expiry date, whether you generate hazardous waste, whether a BIS Quality Control Order covers your product, and whether your machines run on power. A dot on the tab means something is unanswered. The right-hand card shows the Profile answers the rules also read.

## Emissions

Each source of emissions (diesel, LPG, grid electricity, steel bought and so on) is one row with its yearly total, tonnes of CO₂e, where the figure comes from, and the bills as proof. The scope is worked out for you.

| Group | What to enter | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Fuels and refrigerants | diesel, petrol, LPG, PNG, coal, refrigerant top-ups | Scope 1 |
| Electricity | grid electricity from DISCOM bills (kWh); rooftop solar you generate; renewable electricity you buy | Scope 2 (solar adds nothing) |
| Value chain (optional) | steel, aluminium, cement, plastic bought; inbound road freight; business air and rail travel | Scope 3 |

**By month or for the year.** Press **Months** (or **By month**) on a row to open April to March and type each month from your bills; the yearly total is the sum, and the row shows how many months are filled. If you only have one figure for the year, switch on **One yearly total only** and type it in the row.

**Where the figure comes from.** Each row with a figure says **From bills** or **Estimate**; change it if you estimated. An empty row says **Not filled in**; if you don't use that fuel, choose **We don't use this**. The **How good is the data** box shows how much of Scope 1 and 2 comes from bills, which sources are estimated, which have only part of the year, and which are not filled in.

**Proof.** Attach the bills or invoices for each source under **Bills** on its row (PDF or photo, up to 10 MB). A buyer, bank or CA checking your figures will ask for them.

**Last year.** If the previous financial year has figures, each row shows last year's quantity and the change, and **This year and last year** compares Scope 1, Scope 2 and your largest sources. To add last year, choose that year in **Profile** and enter its totals.

**Green power.** Under Electricity, enter **Renewable electricity bought** (kWh) through green energy open access, a green tariff from your DISCOM, or renewable energy certificates (I-RECs). It is part of your grid units, not extra. Scope 2 is then shown two ways, as the GHG Protocol asks: **location-based** (all grid units at the CEA grid average; used for your totals) and **market-based** (the renewable power you bought counts as zero). Keep the open access invoices, green tariff bills or certificate statements. Rooftop solar is always zero-emission.

**Several plants.** List your plants in **Profile → Plants and sites**. With more than one plant, Emissions shows a **Figures for** choice above the groups: pick a plant and enter its figures. Totals add up across plants, and **By plant** shows each plant's Scope 1, Scope 2 and share. With one site nothing changes: everything belongs to your main plant.

The figures at the top show Scope 1, 2 and 3 in tonnes of CO₂e, the change from last year, and your **intensity** (tonnes per ₹ crore of turnover), which buyers use to compare suppliers.

Tip: **Quick tools → Bill scan** reads an electricity or fuel bill PDF and adds it to the yearly total. If a source is entered by month, the amount added this way is shown on the row as "not split by month". The **Bill analyser** can also copy its 12 months of units into grid electricity.

## Reduction plan

Turns your footprint into a plan: which actions to take, what each one cuts in tonnes of CO₂e and saves in rupees, what it costs, and whether together they reach your target.

**Actions.** The **Action library** lists common measures for small Indian factories: LED lighting, variable speed drives, IE3/IE4 motors, compressed-air leaks and pressure, power factor correction, rooftop solar, boiler and furnace efficiency, waste-heat recovery, switching burners to piped gas, running on grid or solar instead of the DG set, and transformer losses. Tap **Add** to put one in your plan, or **Your own action** for anything else.

Open an action to fit it to your site:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Part of your use it touches | the share of that source the action affects, e.g. lighting is about 8% of a factory's electricity |
| Saving on that part | how much of that part it saves (for a switch: how much is switched) |
| How many / Size | number of fittings, kW of motors or kVA, to work out the typical cost |
| Your quoted cost | your supplier's price; leave empty to use the typical cost |
| Owner, date, status | who does it, by when, and idea / planned / in progress / done |

Each action shows tonnes cut a year, rupees saved a year, cost and simple payback. Rooftop solar takes its figures from the **Solar** page and power factor correction from the **Bill analyser**. Attach the quote or invoice under **Quote or invoice**.

The library's figures are **typical and deliberately conservative** (from BEE guide books, EESL programmes and cluster studies). Real savings depend on your site: confirm with an energy audit or a supplier quote before you invest.

**Target and path.** Choose a base year (this year, last year, or an earlier year you enter), a target year and the cut. Two quick choices: **30% by 2030**, or the **1.5 °C path** (about 4.2% less each year, as used by the Science Based Targets initiative). The chart shows emissions with no action, with your plan, and the target line, and says whether you reach it or how many tonnes are still to find. Point at a year to read its figures, or open **Show as a table**.

Only actions marked **planned, in progress or done** count; ideas are listed but not counted. An action done in or before this year is already in your figures, so it is not taken off again. If your answers in ESG say you have no target yet, use **Record this target in ESG**.

**Prices used.** Savings use the energy rate from your power bill and typical fuel prices. Enter what you actually pay for diesel, LPG, PNG or coal to make them yours.

Each financial year keeps its own plan. In a new year, **Bring it into FY …** copies last year's plan.

## ESG

About ten minutes of questions on environment (water, waste, recycling, targets), social (women in the workforce, training, minimum wages, POSH, grievances) and governance (code of conduct, anti-bribery, privacy, supplier code, who is responsible for ESG).

The page shows your **readiness score**, a score for each of Environment, Social and Governance, and your **level**:

| Level | Score |
|---|---|
| 1 Formative | above 30% |
| 2 Developing | above 50% |
| 3 Emerging | above 65% |
| 4 Established | above 80% |

Each section must reach at least 30% for you to be rated. **What would lift your level** lists the quickest gains.

## SRMM (ICAI maturity model)

The ICAI Sustainability Reporting Maturity Model scores your reporting out of 300 points in three sections (A general disclosures, B environment and social, C governance). Manufacturers use version 3.1 (93 indicators), service companies version 3.2. The version is chosen from your Profile; you can change it.

How to score:

1. Filter by section, or show only indicators **not scored**.
2. For each indicator, read the question and the scoring rule, then tap the option that fits. Some indicators have several parts (for example health, accident, maternity and day-care benefits), each scored 0, 1 or 2.
3. Where your data already supports a score, a **suggestion** appears. Accept it with **Use**, or accept all suggestions for unscored indicators at once.
4. Add a remark where it helps the CA reviewing it.

Levels follow the same bands as ESG (above 30 / 50 / 65 / 80%), and each section needs at least 30% of its points.

Your SRMM level becomes official only after a chartered accountant validates it: see **Share → Share & validate**.

## Product footprint

Emissions per product (per tonne, per kg or per 1,000 pieces), for buyers who ask for product carbon footprints. Add each product, its share of your production and its materials. Shares should add up to 100%.

## Certifications

The certifications you hold or are working towards (MSME ZED, ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 50001, 27001 and your own), with status and expiry date. Certificates expiring within 90 days are flagged.
