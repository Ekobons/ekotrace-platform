# Carbon & ESG

This section holds the core of your data: who you are, which rules apply, your carbon footprint, your ESG readiness and your ICAI SRMM level.

## Profile

Your company details (name, manufacturing or services, industry, state, Udyam number) and a few questions about your business:

- **Size:** employees, turnover (₹ crore) and, for manufacturers, yearly production. These give your MSME class (micro, small or medium).
- **Customers and exports:** whether you sell to listed companies, export to the EU or UK, and your main export product.
- **Products:** whether you use plastic packaging, make electrical or electronic goods or batteries.

These answers decide what you see under **What applies** and which pages are tucked away. Only the owner or an admin can change the company details.

The financial year switcher is at the top of this page.

## What applies

The rules that matter for your company, grouped as:

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Act now | applies to you today |
| Check soon | likely to apply, or worth preparing for |
| Keep an eye on | for information; may apply later |
| In order | nothing to do |

Each rule says why it applies and the next step, with a link to the page that helps. Examples: EU and UK CBAM, BRSR value-chain requests from listed customers, plastic EPR, the DPDP Act, POSH, CSR.

> This is guidance to help you prioritise, not legal advice.

## Emissions

Type **yearly totals** from your bills and invoices. The scope is worked out for you.

| Group | What to enter | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Fuels and refrigerants | diesel, petrol, LPG, PNG, coal, refrigerant top-ups | Scope 1 |
| Electricity | grid electricity from DISCOM bills (kWh); rooftop solar you generate | Scope 2 (solar adds nothing) |
| Value chain (optional) | steel, aluminium, plastic bought; road, rail and air freight; business travel | Scope 3 |

Add Scope 3 only if your buyers ask for it. The figures at the top show Scope 1, 2 and 3 in tonnes of CO₂e and your **intensity** (tonnes per ₹ crore of turnover), which buyers use to compare suppliers.

Tip: **Quick tools → Bill scan** reads an electricity or fuel bill PDF and fills the figures for you.

## ESG

About ten minutes of questions on environment (water, waste, recycling, targets), social (women in the workforce, training, minimum wages, POSH, grievances) and governance (code of conduct, anti-bribery, privacy, supplier code, who is responsible for ESG).

The page shows your **readiness score**, a score for each of Environment, Social and Governance, and your **level**:

| Level | Score |
|---|---|
| 1 Formative | above 30% |
| 2 Developing | above 50% |
| 3 Emerging | above 65% |
| 4 Established | above 80% |

Each section must reach at least 30% for you to be rated. **What would lift your level** lists the quickest gains.

## SRMM (ICAI maturity model)

The ICAI Sustainability Reporting Maturity Model scores your reporting out of 300 points in three sections (A general disclosures, B environment and social, C governance). Manufacturers use version 3.1 (93 indicators), service companies version 3.2. The version is chosen from your Profile; you can change it.

How to score:

1. Filter by section, or show only indicators **not scored**.
2. For each indicator, read the question and the scoring rule, then tap the option that fits. Some indicators have several parts (for example health, accident, maternity and day-care benefits), each scored 0, 1 or 2.
3. Where your data already supports a score, a **suggestion** appears. Accept it with **Use**, or accept all suggestions for unscored indicators at once.
4. Add a remark where it helps the CA reviewing it.

Levels follow the same bands as ESG (above 30 / 50 / 65 / 80%), and each section needs at least 30% of its points.

Your SRMM level becomes official only after a chartered accountant validates it: see **Share → Share & validate**.

## Product footprint

Emissions per product (per tonne, per kg or per 1,000 pieces), for buyers who ask for product carbon footprints. Add each product, its share of your production and its materials. Shares should add up to 100%.

## Certifications

The certifications you hold or are working towards (MSME ZED, ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 50001, 27001 and your own), with status and expiry date. Certificates expiring within 90 days are flagged.
