# Save money

Pages that find money: lower electricity bills, rooftop solar, government schemes, selling scrap and carbon credits.

## Bill analyser

Enter one month's HT electricity bill (or read it from the PDF with **Read a bill PDF**):

- contract demand and maximum demand (kVA)
- power factor and any power-factor penalty (₹)
- units billed (kWh) and the share used in peak hours

Choose your electricity company to fill its tariff rates, or leave them empty to use typical rates.

**What we found** lists each saving in plain words, with what to do and the ₹ saved a year, for example:

- *You are paying a penalty for going over your contract demand* — raise the contract demand.
- *Low power factor is adding a penalty* — install capacitors (APFC panel); the cost and payback are shown.
- *You could save by running some load at night* — move flexible work to off-peak hours.

> Savings are estimates from one month's bill. Check a few months before changing your contract demand.

## Solar

How big a rooftop solar system fits your roof and your use, what it costs, what it saves and how fast it pays back. Enter your usable roof area; electricity use comes from **Emissions**. Rates follow your state. Subsidies for factory roofs are explained in words.

## Schemes

Government schemes that fit your company, split into **Likely for you** and **Check first** (a condition such as Udyam registration or MSME class is missing). Each shows what it gives, why it fits, how to apply and the official portal.

## Scrap market

List scrap and waste you want to sell: item, quantity, unit and expected price. The price is **per kg, litre or piece**, even when the quantity is in tonnes or kilolitres. Each listing shows its value.

Statuses: draft → listed → sold (or withdrawn). The Ekotrace team checks listings; a blocked listing shows the reason.

> Hazardous waste can only go to a recycler authorised by your State Pollution Control Board, with a manifest and the yearly Form 4 return.

The recycler directory lists authorised recyclers near you, when your provider has added them.

## Carbon credits

One factory's project is too small to earn carbon credits on its own. Ekotrace pools projects across many SMEs, shares the registration cost and shares the revenue.

Enter new solar capacity or electricity saved to see an estimate. An admin or the owner submits a project: solar (kWp), energy efficiency (kWh saved a year) or fuel switch (diesel no longer burned, litres a year). The Ekotrace team reviews it before it joins the pool; its status is shown in the list.
