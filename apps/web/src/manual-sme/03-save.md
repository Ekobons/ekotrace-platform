# Save money

Pages that find money: lower electricity bills, rooftop solar, government schemes, selling scrap and carbon credits.

## Bill analyser

Choose how many bills to use at the top of the page:

- **Up to 12 months** (recommended). Fill in one row per month, April to March, from your HT electricity bills: units (kWh), maximum demand (kVA), contract demand (kVA; leave it empty if it did not change), power factor, power-factor penalty (₹) and the share of units in peak hours (%). Leave a month empty if you don't have its bill. The **PDF** button on a row reads that month's bill and fills the row for you to check (peak-hour share is not read; take it from the time-of-day section of the bill).
- **One month's bill.** A quick check from a single bill, shown as a yearly figure (× 12).

Choose your electricity company to fill its tariff rates, or leave them empty to use typical rates.

The figures at the top show the savings found a year, how many months went over contract demand, your average (and lowest) power factor, and the power-factor penalty you actually paid.

**What we found** lists each saving in plain words, with what to do and the ₹ saved a year, for example:

- *You went over your contract demand in 3 of 12 months* — raise the contract demand to cover your highest month, or, when it happened in only a few months and raising would cost more, keep the peak down in those months.
- *Low power factor is adding a penalty* — install capacitors (APFC panel), sized for your worst month; the cost and payback are shown.
- *You could save by running some load at night* — move flexible work to off-peak hours.

With fewer than 12 months, each saving is the months you entered scaled to a year (12 ÷ months entered). With all 12, it is what the year actually cost.

**Use these units for grid electricity in Emissions** copies the monthly units into the Emissions page, so you type them once. Attach the bills under **Bills** as proof.

> Savings are estimates from your own bills. A full year gives a much truer picture than one month.

## Solar

How big a rooftop solar system fits your roof and your use, what it costs, what it saves and how fast it pays back. Enter your usable roof area; electricity use comes from **Emissions**. Rates follow your state. Subsidies for factory roofs are explained in words.

## Schemes

Government and bank support that fits your company, split into **Likely for you** and **Check first** (a condition such as Udyam registration, MSME class, sector or "new units only" is not met). The list includes ADEETIE (energy-efficiency loans with interest subvention), ZED certification, the LEAN scheme, CGTMSE collateral-free loans, the Mutual Credit Guarantee Scheme for machinery loans, SIDBI green loans, your state's capital and interest subsidies, rooftop solar and net metering, PMEGP and the CCTS offset pool. Each shows what it gives, why it fits, how to apply and the official portal.

**Tracking an application.** Press **Track an application** under a scheme. Choose the status (Not started, Applied, Sanctioned, Received or Rejected), and add the dates, the reference number and the amounts sanctioned and received. Tick the documents as you get them ready, and attach the application, the sanction letter or the receipt as proof. Tracked schemes appear in **Your applications** at the top, with the totals of support sanctioned and received.

> Scheme terms change. Confirm on the official portal or with your bank or District Industries Centre before you apply.

## Scrap market

List scrap and waste you want to sell: item, quantity, unit and expected price. The price is **per kg, litre or piece**, even when the quantity is in tonnes or kilolitres. Each listing shows its value.

Statuses: draft → listed → sold (or withdrawn). The Ekotrace team checks listings; a blocked listing shows the reason.

> Hazardous waste can only go to a recycler authorised by your State Pollution Control Board, with a manifest and the yearly Form 4 return.

The recycler directory lists authorised recyclers near you, when your provider has added them.

## Carbon credits

One factory's project is too small to earn carbon credits on its own. Ekotrace pools projects across many SMEs, shares the registration cost and shares the revenue.

Enter new solar capacity or electricity saved to see an estimate. An admin or the owner submits a project: solar (kWp), energy efficiency (kWh saved a year) or fuel switch (diesel no longer burned, litres a year). The Ekotrace team reviews it before it joins the pool; its status is shown in the list.
