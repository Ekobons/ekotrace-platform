# Exports

For companies selling to the EU or UK. These pages appear when your Profile says you export there.

## CBAM (EU and UK)

The EU charges importers for the carbon in steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers and hydrogen (CBAM), from 2026. The UK starts its own CBAM on 1 January 2027 (aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen, iron and steel).

Your buyer pays the charge. **Without your data, higher EU default values are used**, so sharing your own figure saves your buyer money and keeps you competitive.

Enter your product, the tonnes you export to the EU and UK, and (if you know them) the carbon price, exchange rate, benchmark and default value; otherwise typical values are used. The page shows:

- your **embedded emissions per tonne** (from your Emissions page),
- the charge with your data compared with default values, and what you save your buyer,
- how the figure is calculated, and the key dates to 2034.

## EUDR

The EU Deforestation Regulation applies to cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood, and products made from them. Choose your commodity, add the **plots** it comes from (location; plots over 4 hectares need a boundary) and tick the five documents buyers need.
